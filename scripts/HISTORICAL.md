# Historical scripts

The following retained scripts are not current PASTED-RACE entry points:

- `train_pasted_race_trace_official_multiseed.sh`: archived fixed seed-42
  trace-head initialization control. It is guarded against accidental launch.
- `run_far_diagnostics.sh`, `run_far_tpr_earlystop.sh`: FAR research branch.
- `run_tuned_bs16_lr20_multiseed.sh`, `run_tuned_bs16_lr25_multiseed.sh`:
  exploratory tuning runs.
- `generate_edu_evidence_labels.sh`: earlier LE-RACE evidence pipeline.
- `diagnose_role_axes.py`, `hroot_role_probe.py`, and hierarchy-similarity
  scripts: completed analysis utilities, retained for provenance.

Creator Retention, P5, and P6 commands.
